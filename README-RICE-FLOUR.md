# EDM Food Shop - Rice, Flour and Eggs

This version supports three product categories:
- Eggs: sold and stocked by Tray (1 Tray = 30 Eggs)
- Rice: sold and stocked by Bag/Package, with package sizes from 1 Kg to 20 Kg
- Flour: sold and stocked by Bag/Package, with package sizes from 1 Kg to 20 Kg

For Rice and Flour, create a product for each package size you sell (for example: Mchele 1 Kg, Mchele 5 Kg, Unga 10 Kg, Unga 20 Kg). Stock quantity is the number of bags/packages of that size.

Sales, purchases, inventory and receipts show the product category and package size.
